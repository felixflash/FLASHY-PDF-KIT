FlashyPDF Kit Privacy Policy
Effective 2026 · Published by FlashCom

Local Processing

Every tool — merge, split, edit, convert, compress — runs entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server. We prioritize your privacy and the security of your documents by ensuring all processing happens locally.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are handled securely by Google Play. We do not have access to your credit card information or personal billing details. All financial transactions are managed by the respective app store platforms.

Advertising

The free version of FlashyPDF Kit uses AdMob to show non-personalized banners. Premium users have all advertising removed. We do not use your personal document data for advertising purposes.

Third-party Libraries

We use open-source libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js, JSZip) that run locally and do not transmit data externally. These libraries are used solely to provide the PDF processing functionality within the app.
